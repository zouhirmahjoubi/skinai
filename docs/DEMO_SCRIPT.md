# Demo video script (target 2:55, hard limit 3:00)

Record on a phone, the device Still Me is built for, using screen recording plus a few seconds of the phone in hand at the mirror. Run the live site with the access code, so analysis, try-ons and the companion are live. Use no music, or royalty-free music you have the rights to. Don't show third-party logos or brands: the store in the app is fictional, and any wig, hat or earring photo you use must be your own or licensed.

Before recording:
- Open the live site once a few minutes earlier, so the free server is awake.
- Have ready: a selfie, a head-and-shoulders portrait, a photo of the top of your head with your parting showing, one wig photo of a model facing forward (JPEG, 1024 px or less) and one earring product photo.
- Load the sample journey in a second browser profile, so you can cut to week 3.
