# Demo video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Record on a phone (the device Ahead is built for) with screen recording, plus a few seconds of the phone in hand at the mirror. Run the server **with your YouCam key** so the try-ons and analysis are live. Use no music, or royalty-free music you have the rights to. Don't show third-party logos or brands; the store in the app is fictional.

Before recording: load the sample journey once on a second browser profile, so you can cut between your live baseline and the three-week sample.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Today screen of the sample journey, road-ahead timeline | "When you start cancer treatment, you're told your skin, hair and brows will change. Nobody tells you what that will look like, which changes matter, or what's safe to buy. Ahead does." |
| 0:15–0:40 | Fresh setup: choose *EGFR inhibitor* + *Chemotherapy*, start date in 3 days, consent | "You tell it your treatment and start date. Everything stays on your phone. Photos only go to the YouCam API when you ask for an analysis." |
| 0:40–1:05 | Baseline check-in: oval guide, light check, capture, steps, results with scores; tap a score to show the YouCam mask | "Your baseline selfie goes through YouCam AI Skin Analysis: eight signals, HD or SD depending on the photo, with detection masks. Ahead frames your face so YouCam's size requirement is always met, and records the light, so later photos are comparable." |
| 1:05–1:40 | Switch to sample journey, week 3: red *Tell your care team* note, then Shelf with benzoyl peroxide under *Not right now* | "Three weeks in, the clear-skin score drops 17 points. A normal skin app would sell you acne cream. On an EGFR inhibitor, that's the drug's rash, and acne products can make it worse. Ahead tells you to call your care team, and the shelf blocks acne actives and fragrance, with the exact ingredient highlighted. Small dips stay 'steady': Ahead ignores changes within normal photo-to-photo noise." |
| 1:40–2:20 | Hair & brows: make the print (colour swatches, face shape, brow shape), try a wig template with *Keep my natural colour*, drag the compare slider, paste a shop's wig image URL | "Before hair changes, one selfie saves your Hair & Brow Print using YouCam's Facial Color Tones and Face Attributes analysis. Then try wig styles on yourself with YouCam Hairstyle Try-On, from templates or any shop's wig photo, in your own colour." |
| 2:20–2:40 | Brow preview (makeup VTO) and the fitting sheet | "If your brows thin, YouCam Makeup Try-On draws your own measured brow shape and colour back on, with retouching switched off. Everything goes on a fitting sheet for your wig specialist, alongside an insurance letter." |
| 2:40–2:55 | Care-team summary, print preview | "And before each appointment, a one-page summary for your care team. Ahead: stay a step ahead of what treatment changes." |

YouCam APIs to name aloud: AI Skin Analysis, Facial Color Tones Analyzer, Face Attributes Analyzer, Hairstyle Virtual Try-On, Makeup Virtual Try-On (eyebrows).
